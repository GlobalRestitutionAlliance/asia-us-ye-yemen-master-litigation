Yemen

The ICRC has been in Yemen since 1962 and has a permanent presence in several governorates. Since the outbreak of the armed conflict in Yemen in 2015, the ICRC has been assisting those affected by it by providing clean water, health care and other relief.

Our work in Yemen

Years of armed conflict, other violence and economic hardship have exhausted people's capacity to cope and driven all essential services nearly to the breaking point. The humanitarian situation is worsening, with 70% of the population reliant on humanitarian aid to survive and more than 80% of Yemenis living below the poverty line (2023 figures).

The ICRC has been in Yemen since 1962, providing aid in collaboration with the Yemen Red Crescent Society. We focus on projects improving health, water and sanitation in vulnerable communities; helping people to become financially independent; organizing the return of detainees; and clearing areas of explosive remnants of war.

The impact of our work - January to June, 2026

681,013

Consultations (including 26,957 antenatal care consultations) were conducted in all ICRC-supported health facilities

62

Health facilities (primary health centers, hospitals, Detention Clinics, and casualty collection points) received material, technical, and financial support

334,284

People benefited from various ICRC economic security support, including food rations, essential household items, unconditional cash assistance, livelihood cash grants and vouchers, and livestock vaccination

1,720,125

Livestock benefited from ICRC-supported vaccination and treatment campaigns, benefiting over 287,686 livestock keepers

181,406

People benefited from various ICRC water and habitat activities and programs in the Water and Sanitation sectors, through the construction & rehabilitation of 10 projects supporting the water and sanitation local corporations

944,577

Square metres of land contaminated by landmines and explosive ordnance was cleared by the Yemen Executive Mine Action Centre (YEMAC) in northern and southern Yemen with the technical and financial support of the ICRC
